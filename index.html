About the Debutante

Elaine

Eighteen Years Old

Ask anyone who knows Elaine, and chances are, they’ll mention her laugh first. It’s the kind you can hear from across the room. She’s stubborn in the best way, determined enough to get things done, and somehow always finds time for the people she loves, even when she forgets to make time for herself. Now that she’s eighteen, she has grown in so many ways, but one thing hasn’t changed. She’s still just as passionate and opinionated about her music as she was when she was eight.
